Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later? expand_more
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. If you upgrade, the new features will be available immediately and your next billing cycle will reflect the pro-rated difference.
What document types are supported? expand_more
We currently support PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Markdown files. Our Enterprise plan also supports proprietary knowledge base integrations via API and direct database connectors.
How secure is my data? expand_more
Security is our core pillar. All documents are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit via TLS. We do not use your private data to train our global models unless explicitly permitted in our Enterprise agreements.
